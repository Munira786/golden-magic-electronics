Our Story

Building trusted UAE technology systems since 2005

Founded in 2005, Golden Magic Electronics began as a trusted electronics solutions provider in Dubai. Over the years, the company expanded into enterprise IT infrastructure, CCTV surveillance, networking, structured cabling, and managed technology services.

Today, GME supports businesses across the UAE with secure, scalable, and reliable technology solutions. Our focus remains on delivering practical systems that improve operations, strengthen security, and support long-term growth.
