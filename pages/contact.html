Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding our services and support.

How can I request a quotation?

You can request a quotation by filling out the form on this page, sending us an email at gme786@gmail.com, or directly messaging us on WhatsApp with your project requirements.

Do you provide UAE-wide support?

Yes, while our head office is in Dubai, we provide installation and on-site support services across all Emirates including Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, and Ajman.

Do you offer Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)?

Absolutely. We offer comprehensive AMCs for IT infrastructure, CCTV systems, servers, and telephony to ensure continuous operation and preventive care.

Can I request a site survey?

Yes, we provide professional site surveys to accurately assess your networking, cabling, or surveillance requirements before delivering a finalized proposal.

How quickly can support requests be handled?

We prioritize urgent infrastructure issues. Depending on your SLA or AMC contract, emergency response can be immediate remotely, or within a few hours for on-site visits in Dubai.

Do you provide remote technical support?

Yes, for server, network configuration, and software-related issues, our team can provide secure remote technical support to resolve problems instantly.
